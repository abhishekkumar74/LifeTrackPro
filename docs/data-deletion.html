LifeTrack Pro is committed to protecting your privacy and giving you total control over your personal information. In compliance with Google Play Developer Policy, this page describes how you can delete your account and associated data at any time, both from inside the app and through a web request.

Summary: You can delete your account instantly from within the LifeTrack Pro app or submit a deletion request below using your registered email address. All your personal data, focus sessions, journal notes, and habit history will be permanently deleted within 30 days.

1. Method 1: In-App Account Deletion (Instant)

If you have the LifeTrack Pro app installed on your Android device, you can delete your account directly:

  1. Open the LifeTrack Pro app on your device.
  2. Tap the Profile icon on the bottom navigation bar.
  3. Tap Account Settings or scroll to the Danger Zone section.
  4. Select Schedule Account Deletion or Delete Account.
  5. Confirm your choice in the confirmation dialog.

Once confirmed, your active session is immediately terminated, and your account and data are queued for permanent deletion.

2. Method 2: Web Deletion Request (Without App Installed)

If you have uninstalled the app or cannot access your device, you can submit an account or data deletion request directly via email:

Submit Deletion Request

Enter your registered account email address to generate a pre-formatted deletion request:

Send Deletion Request Email

3. Types of Data Deleted vs. Retained

When an account deletion request is processed, the following actions are taken on your data stored in our cloud infrastructure (Supabase):

Data Category What It Includes Retention Status
Account Information Email address, username, profile category, user settings Permanently Deleted
Focus & Productivity Logs Focus sessions, timer durations, subjects, earned XP points, streaks Permanently Deleted
Habit & Routine Tracking Daily habit completions, schedules, milestone records Permanently Deleted
Journal & Notes Encrypted diary entries, gratitude notes, mood tracking Permanently Deleted
Syllabus Progress Exam syllabus trackers, chapter completions, revision schedules Permanently Deleted
Diagnostics & Crash Telemetry Anonymous error logs (Sentry/PostHog) Anonymized / Purged

4. Data Retention and Processing Period

Following your deletion request:

  • Immediate Action: Your account access is immediately suspended, and data is hidden from all public/leaderboard services.
  • Grace Period & Permanent Erasure: All database records are permanently deleted from our primary servers within 48 hours. Complete deletion across all encrypted backup snapshots occurs within a maximum of 30 days.
  • No Additional Retention: LifeTrack Pro does not retain any identifying user data after the 30-day window.

5. Request Partial Data Deletion

If you wish to retain your account but delete specific datasets (e.g. wipe your study history or reset habit logs), you can either:

  • Use the in-app data reset options under Settings → Storage and Reset.
  • Select "Delete Study, Focus, and Habit Logs Only" in the request form above.
