## 🎨 3. STRICT VISUAL DESIGN SYSTEM (Design Tokens)

When prompting image generators or designing graphics, **ALWAYS enforce this exact color palette and aesthetic**.

### A. Color Palette (Strict HEX Codes)
* **Primary Background (Dark / Hero)**: `#0F172A` (Obsidian Slate) / `#17172A` (Deep Navy)
* **Primary Background (Light / Clean)**: `#F7F6F3` (Warm Ivory Cream) / `#FFFFFF` (Pure Card Surface)
* **Primary Brand Accent (Electric Violet)**: `#5B4FE8` *(Use for primary buttons, glow effects, active rings, main highlights)*
* **Violet Tint / Soft Glow**: `#EAE8FD` (or `rgba(91, 79, 232, 0.15)`)
* **Success / Progress Accent (Mint Emerald)**: `#00B894` *(Use for completed tasks, on-track badges, syllabus progress)*
* **Streak & Alert Accent (Amber Flame)**: `#E8A020` *(Use for flame streaks, milestone highlights, urgent tags)*
* **Warning / Blocker Accent (Coral Red)**: `#E85858` *(Use for errors, missed blocks, app blocker active)*
* **Text Colors**:
  * Dark Mode Text: `#FFFFFF` (Headings) / `#94A3B8` (Muted Subtext)
  * Light Mode Text: `#17172A` (Deep Navy Primary) / `#5C5C70` (Body Text)

### B. Typography & Font Style
* **Display / Hero Headlines**: `Instrument Serif` *(Editorial, elegant, vintage prestige, high-trust academic feel)*
* **UI / Body / Subheadings**: `DM Sans` *(Clean, crisp, geometric modern sans-serif)*
* **Timers / Stats / Badges**: `DM Mono` *(Technical, terminal-like, razor-sharp numbers for countdowns & streak counts)*

### C. Visual Aesthetic & Vibe (Look & Feel)
* **Design Philosophy**: *"Linear meets Editorial Vintage"*. 
* Minimalist, dark-mode first, sleek obsidian cards with subtle 1px border (`#1E293B` or `#E2E8F0`).
* Soft ambient neon violet (`#5B4FE8`) backlight glow behind screens/mockups.
* Clean glassmorphic badges, high-contrast typography, zero clutter.
* **FORBIDDEN (Never Use)**:
  * ❌ NO cartoonish 3D bubble characters.
  * ❌ NO cheap bright rainbow gradients.
  * ❌ NO generic corporate electric blue (`#0066FF`).
  * ❌ NO cluttered, busy collage layouts.
