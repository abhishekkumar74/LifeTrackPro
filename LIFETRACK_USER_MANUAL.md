# LifeTrack Pro — Ultimate User Manual & Pitch Deck Outline

Welcome to **LifeTrack Pro**, a premium, productivity-focused mobile application designed specifically for students and achievers (with custom onboarding and syllabus roadmaps for Computer Science / CSE students).

This manual acts as a step-by-step onboarding guide for users, as well as a structured presentation outline (pitch deck) that you can use to pitch the app to prospective users, colleges, or investors.

## Part 1: Product Overview & Pitch Deck (Slides Outline)

Use this outline to create a pitch presentation (PowerPoint / Google Slides / Canva).

### Slide 1: Title & Vision
* **Title**: LifeTrack Pro: From Daily Hustle to Career Accomplishment
* **Subtitle**: A personalized, structured ecosystem mapping daily focus directly to your long-term career goals.
* **Visual suggestion**: App home screen screenshot mockup.

### Slide 2: The Problem (The "Chao" of Modern Students)
* **Scattered Information**: Students use one app for note-taking, another for to-do lists, another for focus timers, and syllabus PDFs are forgotten in downloads folder.

* **Connected Goals**: 3-Tier hierarchy connects daily tasks directly to milestones and high-level goals.
* **Dynamic Pacing**: Recommends study pacing (e.g., "Do 3 topics/day") and counts down days until readiness.

### Slide 4: Target Audience & Custom Journeys
* **CSE/IT Students**: Pre-loaded roadmaps (FAANG SDE prep, Gate Prep, Backend, Full-stack) mapping subjects (DSA, OS, Database, Networking, etc.) directly to learning checkpoints.
* **General Achievers**: Custom timelines for board exams, competitive prep, and personal growth.

## Part 2: Step-by-Step User Manual

### Phase 1: Onboarding & Setup (Day 1)
1. **Choose Category**: Select your academic focus. Choosing **CSE Student** initializes the special computer science roadmap.
2. **Select Career Goals**: Choose subcategories (e.g., *Amazon SDE, Google SWE, Gate Exam, Web Dev*) to tailor dashboard suggestion banners.
3. **Choose Primary Goal**: Setup your ultimate milestone goal (e.g., *Crack Amazon Interview in 6 Months*).

---

### Phase 2: Mastering the 3-Tier Goal System
This is the core engine of LifeTrack Pro. Explain this clearly to users.

```
+-------------------------------------------------------------+
| 1. Goal (e.g., "Crack Google SWE Interview" - 6 Months)     |
+-------------------------------------------------------------+
                              |
                              v (Divided into)
+-------------------------------------------------------------+
| 2. Milestones (e.g., "Phase 1: Master DSA" - Due in 30 Days) |
+-------------------------------------------------------------+
                              |
                              v (Supported by)
+-------------------------------------------------------------+
| 3. Actionable Tasks (e.g., "Solve 5 Tree Questions today")  |
+-------------------------------------------------------------+
```

* **Action Item**: Add milestones to your active goal. Under each milestone, create small tasks with due dates. Completed tasks automatically update the goal progress ring on your Home dashboard.

---

### Phase 3: Daily Dashboard Workflow (Day-to-Day Use)
Every morning, the user opens the app and sees:
1. **Today's Focus**: Displays the suggested topic you should study based on your syllabus progress (e.g., *Practice: Binary Trees*). Pressing **Start** opens the Focus Timer pre-filled with this subject.
2. **Today's Tasks**: Check off daily items directly with a swipe or tap (integrated with satisfying Haptic feedback). Add new tasks on the fly.
3. **Today's Schedule**: Displays your time slots (e.g., *12:00 PM - DSA Practice*). You can add new routines/slots instantly using the `+ Add block` button in the header.
4. **Habits Tracker**: Tick off daily micro-habits (*Drink 8 glasses of water*, *Wake up early*, *Study 4 hours*) to build and maintain streak multipliers.

---

### Phase 4: Learn Screen (Syllabus & Spaced Repetition)
The **Learn** tab houses the academic brain:
1. **Syllabus Tracker**: Displays active subjects. Shows completion percentage (0% to 100%) and chapter progress.
2. **Syllabus Expansion**: Tap a subject to expand chapters, tap a chapter to view individual topics, and change topic status (*Not Started*, *In Progress*, *Done*).
3. **Study Notes**: Tap a topic to write quick-markdown study notes.
4. **Flashcards & Spaced Repetition**: Notes are converted into flashcards automatically. The app flags cards that are due for revision based on memory retention intervals. Use the active review sessions to check your recall.

---

### Phase 5: Focus Tab (The Pomodoro Engine)
When it's time to study:
1. Navigate to the **Focus** tab.
2. Pick a subject from the Horizontal scroll select chips (e.g., *DSA, Java, Backend*).
3. Select an optional background ambiance track (White noise, Lo-fi, Rainfall).
4. Start the session. Once the timer finishes, it will prompt you with a checklist of incomplete syllabus topics in that subject.
5. Select the topics you completed during this session, and the app will automatically mark them as **Done** in the database.
