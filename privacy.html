Welcome to LifeTrack Pro. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy describes how LifeTrack Pro ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and safeguards your information when you use our mobile application available on the Google Play Store.

Core Promise: We do not sell your personal information. Your private notes, journal reflections, and focus session logs are encrypted and never shared with third-party advertisers for data brokerage.

1. Information We Collect

We collect only the information necessary to provide you with core productivity, habit tracking, and focus timer features:

  • Account Credentials: When you register or sign in, we collect your email address and profile username. Account authentication is handled securely via Supabase Auth.
  • Study & Focus Activity: Focus session durations, accuracy ratings, subject tags, timestamps, and earned XP points to calculate your productivity statistics and streaks.
  • Personal Vault & Journal Entries: Diary entries, gratitude reflections, and mood check-ins created in the Secret Journal Vault. This content is encrypted and protected with a local 4-digit PIN.
  • Syllabus & Task Data: Chapters, study topics, checklist items, and scheduled routine blocks that you choose to track.
  • Diagnostic & Device Information: Technical details such as device model, OS version, battery state (for live timer background sync), and anonymous crash telemetry via Sentry to resolve bugs.
  • Advertising Identifier: Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) may collect the device's Google Advertising ID (AAID) strictly to serve voluntary rewarded video ads requested by the user.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following legitimate business purposes:

  • To authenticate your account and securely sync your study data across devices.
  • To generate personal productivity charts, weekly consistency heatmaps, and achievement badges.
  • To deliver optional local reminders, streak protection alerts, and study notifications.
  • To credit bonus XP and streak shields when you voluntarily choose to watch a rewarded ad.
  • To diagnose crashes and improve the performance and reliability of the app.

3. Third-Party Service Providers

To provide high-quality cloud synchronization, crash reporting, and ad delivery, LifeTrack Pro integrates trusted third-party services. Each provider operates under strict privacy safeguards:

  • Supabase: Cloud database, authentication, and encrypted data storage. (Supabase Privacy Policy)
  • Google AdMob & Google Play Services: Delivers opt-in rewarded ads and app distribution. (Google Privacy Policy)
  • Sentry: Real-time crash reporting and stability diagnostics. (Sentry Privacy Policy)
  • PostHog: Privacy-friendly, anonymous feature usage analytics to understand user adoption. (PostHog Privacy Policy)

4. Data Storage and Security

We implement industry-standard administrative, technical, and physical security measures:

  • All communication between the LifeTrack Pro app and backend servers is encrypted in transit using Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3 / HTTPS).
  • Database records are protected with Row Level Security (RLS), ensuring that only authenticated owners can read or write their own data.
  • Your Secret Journal Vault is additionally safeguarded on your device with your personal 4-digit PIN lock.

5. Account & Data Deletion (User Rights)

You have full ownership of your data and can request complete, permanent deletion at any time:

  • In-App Deletion: You can delete your account instantly from within the app by going to: Profile → Account Settings → Danger Zone → Schedule Account Deletion.
  • Online Deletion Page: You can submit an online request directly on our Account & Data Deletion Page.
  • Manual Email Request: If you cannot access the app, you can submit an account deletion request by emailing abhishekkumar86448@gmail.com with the subject line "Account Deletion Request". Please provide your registered account email. All your personal data, journal vaults, study sessions, and history will be permanently erased from our servers within 30 days.

6. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K Compliance)

LifeTrack Pro is designed for high school and university students, working professionals, and lifelong learners. The app is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately, and we will promptly delete such records.
