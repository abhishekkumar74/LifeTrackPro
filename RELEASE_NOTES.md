# Release Notes — LifeTrack Pro v1.0.0

Welcome to the production launch of **LifeTrack Pro**! This initial release brings together personal productivity, study goal management, community focus rooms, and spaced repetition notes in one unified, type-safe mobile application.

## Key Features

### 1. Goal & Milestone Management
- **Goal Vault**: Set, track, and archive primary and secondary goals.
- **Native Milestone Creation**: Add milestones directly to goals from the Milestones tab or from inside Goal Cards.
- **Task Tracking**: Drag to organize and swipe to complete tasks associated with milestones.

### 2. Habit Building & Streak Tracking
- **Default Habits Seeding**: Automatically get 5 pre-configured habits (wake up, drink water, exercise, study focus, read) upon onboarding.
- **Streak Tracker**: Keep a visual daily check-in streak with automatic local notifications when you are close to breaking your streak.

### 3. Community Focus Rooms
- **Real-time Study Rooms**: Create or join silent, music, or discussion rooms with live presence indicators and real-time chat broadcast.
- **Integrated Timer**: Run study focus blocks with other members in the room.

### 4. Spaced Repetition Learn Vault
- **Syllabus Planner**: Pre-warm syllabus topics for competitive exams (NEET, JEE, UPSC, SSC).
- **Spaced Repetition Notes**: Concepts are automatically scheduled for active recall revisions based on quality scoring (SM-2 algorithm).
- **Flashcard Review**: Flip cards to revise due items with native swipe gestures.

### 5. Premium UX Enhancements
- **Skeleton Placeholders**: Smooth pulsating loaders replace generic loading indicators.
- **Offline Mode Indicator**: A sliding alert banner warns users when internet connectivity is lost, enabling local data reference.
- **Haptic Interventions**: Tactile feedback on primary button presses, checklist toggles, and status updates.
- **Hardware Integration**: Hardware back button interceptors for seamless Android navigation.
